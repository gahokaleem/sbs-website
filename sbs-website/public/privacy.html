Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the SBS (Shershah, Bolton & Saddar) app and website collect and how it is used. Last updated: .

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the name, phone number, and delivery address you provide, along with the products and quantities you ordered. We do not require you to create an account or provide any other personal information to browse or order.

How we use it

We use this information only to fulfil and deliver your order, and to contact you about it by phone or WhatsApp. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data storage

Order and product information is stored in our database to operate the store and to let you look up your own past orders by phone number.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to us at 0345 6713007 or via WhatsApp.
